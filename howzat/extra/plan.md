## A: "Muscle kaise banta hai?" (35 s, dark theme)
Spoken script (tags removed): Muscle gym me nahi... gym ke BAAD banta hai! Heavy set me fibres pe tension aata
hai. Andar ke sensors isse alarm samajhte hain, aur mTOR switch on ho jaata hai. Teen ghante baad protein banne ki
speed double se zyada, aur do din tak upar! Isi time body protein jod ke fibres mote karti hai. Roz har kilo body
weight pe 1.6 gram protein khao. Growth chalu rakhni hai? Har hafte thoda zyada: ek rep ya thoda weight, dono
chalega. Ye reel us dost ko bhejo jo saal bhar same weight uthata hai.
Facts and sources:
- Muscle protein synthesis after one heavy session: +112% at 3 h, +65% at 24 h, +34% at 48 h.
  Phillips SM et al. (1997), Am J Physiol Endocrinol Metab 273(1): E99–E107.
- Benefit of protein plateaus around 1.6 g per kg body weight per day (meta-analysis, 49 studies).
  Morton RW et al. (2018), Br J Sports Med 52(6): 376–384. doi:10.1136/bjsports-2017-097608
- Adding reps grew muscle about as well as adding weight over 8 weeks (43 trained lifters).
  Plotkin D et al. (2022), PeerJ 10:e14142. doi:10.7717/peerj.14142

## B: "Full squat ya half squat?" (1 min 49 s, dark theme)
Gist: full squats (~140° knee) vs half squats (90°), 17 untrained young men, 2×/week for 10 weeks, MRI.
Glutes +6.7% vs +2.2% (about 3×), adductors +6.2% vs +2.7% (more than 2×), quads +4.9% vs +4.6% (same).
Hamstrings barely changed in either group. Caveats: one small study, beginners, 10 weeks.
Verdict: for bigger glutes squat as deep as you can with good form; half squats are an extra, not the main squat.
Source: Kubo K, Ikebukuro T, Yata H (2019), Eur J Appl Physiol 119(9): 1933–1942. doi:10.1007/s00421-019-04181-y
